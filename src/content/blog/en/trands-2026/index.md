The real estate market in 2026 is undergoing a clear shift. After years of rapid price growth, we're now seeing a period of stabilization, with the focus moving away from simple ownership toward long-term quality, location, and running costs. Both individual and institutional investors are increasingly asking not "how much will this cost to buy," but "how much will this cost to maintain over the next twenty years."

![Market growth chart](./wykres-rynku.jpg)

## The growing importance of location

Location has always been a key factor in property valuation, but in 2026 we're defining it differently than we did even five years ago. Proximity to a city centre is no longer the only measure of desirability — access to public transport, green spaces, and infrastructure that supports remote and hybrid work now carries significant weight.

Investors are paying particularly close attention to locations that combine a quieter setting with strong transport links — suburban towns with direct rail or road connections to larger hubs. These are exactly the areas where we're currently seeing the strongest interest.

## Energy efficiency as standard, not an add-on

Not long ago, a high energy rating was mainly a marketing point. Today it's becoming a baseline requirement. Rising energy costs and tightening regulations mean that low-energy-demand properties now hold a clear advantage — both for buyers purchasing a home to live in and for investors calculating rental returns.

Heat pumps, solar panels, and quality insulation are no longer premium extras — they're becoming the standard buyers expect when comparing offers from different developers.

## Flexible apartment layouts

The approach to apartment design itself is also changing. Rigid, fixed layouts are giving way to solutions that can easily adapt to residents' changing needs — remote work, a growing family, or renting out part of the space. Flexible zones, additional multi-purpose rooms, and thoughtful natural light are becoming genuine selling points.

## What this means for investors

For anyone planning to buy an investment property in 2026, the key takeaways are:

- favour locations with strong transport links, even at the cost of greater distance from the centre,
- energy efficiency directly affects both rental appeal and resale value,
- flexible layouts widen the pool of potential tenants and buyers,
- think in terms of decades, not quarters, when it comes to investment horizon.
